Before you book

The questions we get asked most

How long is the call?

Around 30 minutes. We keep it tight and useful — you'll know inside the first ten whether this is worth continuing.

Is this just a sales pitch?

No. We map out what we'd actually do, and you keep that plan whether or not you ever hire us.

What should I prepare?

Nothing formal. A rough idea of your monthly lead volume and where deals stall is plenty.

Can I reschedule?

Any time. Your confirmation email has a reschedule link — one click, no need to message us.

Who will I be speaking to?

Melvin, directly. Not a call centre and not a junior setter reading a script.

What if none of the times work?

Scroll the calendar forward — more slots open further out. Still stuck? Reply to any of our emails and we'll find one.
